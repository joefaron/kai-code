Boat Plan

Where everything goes on the boat, how it steers, and the choices that are yours to make. Pages with an orange letter are choices: read the options, test if you can, tick one, and write down why. The judges will ask.

Wiring on the breadboard itself is in the Build Steps booklet. Sizes used in these drawings: deck 40 × 23 cm, bottles 20 cm apart, prop 70 mm, pontoons = 8 small bottles each. Measure yours and tell Bugsy to change them.

1

Page 1The whole boat from above

Front of the boat is at the top. "Left" and "right" always mean the boat's own left and right, as if you're sitting on it looking forward. Two fans at the back, one behind each bottle, blow air backwards. Nothing hangs in the water except the bottles.

🧠 Why this layout: the fans are at the back, so the heavy power bank goes at the front to balance them. The dry box sits in the middle, away from spray. This is a starting point: choices A and B change it.

2

Page 2From the side: fans hang off the back

Each prop blade reaches 3.5 cm from the motor shaft, but the holder's flat base is only 1.6 cm below the shaft. Flat on the deck, the blades would hit it. So the holder sits right on the back edge, with the motor pointing backwards: the prop spins behind the boat, where there's no deck to hit.

🔧 Mounting: one small screw (#4 × ½") through the hole in the holder's flat base into the deck, plus hot glue so it can't twist. Snap the motor in with its shaft pointing backwards. Stick the two holders at the same distance from the edge so the fans match.

✅ Check: spin each prop slowly by hand: it doesn't touch the deck, a bottle or a wire. Float the boat and measure how high the lowest blade tip is above the water: ______ cm. Less than 2 cm? Waves will splash it, so move the holder up (a small block under it).

It steers like a tank: push one side harder and the boat swings the other way. The code does it for you when you push the joystick sideways. Fans further apart = stronger turning (like pushing a door far from its hinges).

🧪 Test and write it down: these props push much less air spinning backwards, so reverse and spinning on the spot will be weak. How long does a full circle take? Try it in the tub.

👻 Real Everglades airboats use a rudder. Ours has zero moving parts except the fans. Tell the judges that.

5

Prototype 1Is it fast enough? Measure, then decide

Prototype 1 is supposed to teach you something. Two numbers tell you what to change:

  1. Boat weight: everything on it, on a kitchen scale: ______ g
  2. Thrust per fan: tape a motor + prop to a block blowing air up, stand it on the scale, zero it, switch on. The reading goes up by the thrust: ______ g
If you see thisIt meansTry this next
Crawls, but steersNot enough pushFirst try 6 AA (9 V) and 4 fans (see the 4-fan page). Still slow? Bigger motors (130 size) with bigger props.
Sits deep in the waterToo heavy, or the floats are too smallLess tape, lighter parts. Deep floats drag a lot of water.
Bumpy tape on the floatsTape edges catch waterSmooth tape, running front to back.
Stops in a light breezeWind pushes the tall partsKeep everything low. Point it into the wind for the start.

⚡ Batteries: our motors are rated 3 to 12 V, but the motor driver can take at most 10.8 V. Each AA is 1.5 V: 4 AA = 6 V works, 6 AA = 9 V spins the fans about 1.5× faster (about 2× the push), and 8 AA = 12 V breaks the driver. Test it: measure thrust at 6 V and at 9 V.

👻 A heavy boat isn't doomed: weight makes it slow to start. Top speed is decided by push vs drag.

The driver chip has only 2 channels (left and right), so the two fans on each side share one. Holes in the same column are joined inside the breadboard, so i8 is the same wire as j8. No code change: both fans on a side get the same signal, and steering still works like a tank. The code can't tell if the extra fans are plugged in, and it doesn't need to.

📏 Space: four 7 cm props need about 30 cm, and the deck is 23 cm, so the outer fans hang a little past the sides. Bigger props? Widen the deck, or they'll hit each other.

✅ Check: all 4 fans blow backwards. One blows forward? Swap its two wires at the WAGO clip. Test with 6 AA (9 V): 4 fans pull twice the current, and the driver can handle it.
